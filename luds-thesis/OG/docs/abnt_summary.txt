The document ABNT NBR 5419-1:2015 - Proteção contra Descargas Atmosféricas, Parte 1: Princípios Gerais provides comprehensive guidelines for protecting structures and systems against lightning strikes. Here's a summary:

Key Areas Covered:
Scope and Applicability:

Establishes general principles for determining and applying lightning protection for structures.
Excludes systems like railways, vehicles, aircraft, ships, offshore platforms, and high-pressure underground pipes, as these follow specialized regulations.
Definitions:

Detailed definitions of terms related to lightning phenomena (e.g., types of lightning strikes, components of a lightning stroke, current parameters).
Terms like "downward flash," "upward flash," "lightning protection zone (LPZ)," and "protection measures" are precisely defined to standardize understanding.
Damage Types and Risk:

Lightning strikes can cause structural damage, fires, electrical surges, and risks to human life.
The document categorizes risks to structures based on function and contents (e.g., residential buildings, schools, hospitals, industries, and cultural sites) and outlines the specific risks each category faces from lightning events.
Protection Measures:

Emphasizes a multi-faceted approach involving external lightning protection systems (SPDA) and internal measures to ensure safety.
Includes the use of Franklin rods, Faraday cages, and grounding systems.
Advises on reducing risks to internal electrical and electronic systems, with specialized surge protection measures and grounding strategies.
Lightning Protection Levels (LPL):

Introduces protection levels I through IV, each corresponding to specific parameters of lightning current.
Each level defines acceptable current strength and surge durations, helping designers tailor protection systems to the building’s risk profile and environmental factors.
Risk Management:

The document cross-references ABNT NBR 5419-2, which provides a risk management approach, advising on when and where lightning protection is economically and practically beneficial.
Annexes:

Provides technical data and parameters for lightning simulation, testing, and system design in annexes (A through E), covering everything from current waveform parameters to effects on structures and recommended testing standards for SPDA components.
Intended Outcomes:
By implementing these guidelines, structures can significantly mitigate risks associated with lightning strikes, protecting both the physical building and its occupants or systems within. The document is intended for professionals involved in electrical and structural engineering and lightning protection system design.
